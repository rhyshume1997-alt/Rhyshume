Privacy

Who we are

Marpet Fabrications (1997) Ltd (registered in Scotland, SC172812) is responsible for the information you give us through this website. You can reach us at Unit 4 Clyde Workshops, Fullarton Road, Glasgow, G32 8YL, on 0141 641 1778, or at info@marpet.co.uk.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect your name, your email address and what you tell us about the job. Your company, phone number and the type of work are optional.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

We use what you send to answer your enquiry, to prepare a quotation, and to carry out any work that follows. We rely on our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us, and on taking steps you have asked for before entering into a contract.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is read by Marpet staff. It is delivered to us by email, and the form is checked for spam, so the companies that provide those services handle it on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any work that follows, and for as long as the law requires us to keep business records.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email info@marpet.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk (opens in a new tab).
