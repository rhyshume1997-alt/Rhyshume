About Marpet

Sheet metal fabricators to the east of Glasgow, serving food processing, packaging and catering.

The company

Where we are and who we work for

Marpet Fabrications is located to the east of Glasgow, placed to serve local customers and those further afield by the M74.

Marpet is an established supplier to food processing, packaging and catering, working with companies locally and nationally.

To confirm Founding date and company history. The existing site says "established in the early 80's". Company registry listings show Marpet Fabrications (1997) Ltd incorporated in February 1997.

How we work

Workshop or on site

The work is produced by skilled, time served tradesmen in a fully equipped fabrication workshop, or on site at client premises.

Marpet will design, manufacture and install on site virtually all types of stainless steel, mild steel or aluminium fabrication.
