Computer system validation for regulated manufacturers

I’m a CSV Engineer at Terumo Aortic, validating computer systems for medical device manufacturing. I write and run validation for computerised systems: plans, risk assessments, IQ/OQ/PQ, traceability, data migration and periodic review.

1.0 Scope of work

For manufacturers working under a quality system, a computerised system has to be validated. It also has to stay validated as the business and the system change.

RefDeliverableWhat it covers
1.1Validation planScoped to the system, its intended use and your quality system, so everyone agrees what validated means before testing starts
1.2Risk assessmentRisk-based thinking that shows where testing effort needs to go, for product quality and data integrity
1.3IQ/OQ/PQ protocolsWritten and executed, with results recorded as the testing happens
1.4Traceability matrixRequirements linked to tests, so you can show that every requirement was tested
1.5Data migrationValidation of records moved from one system to another
1.6Validation reportA clear summary of what was tested, what was found and the outcome, for your QA team to review
1.7Periodic reviewChecking validated systems still match how they’re used, so they stay in a validated state as things change

2.0 Background

At Terumo Aortic the work covers protocols, data migration, project work and periodic reviews.

Before that I worked in chemistry and the labs, through method development, QC and QA. Then I ran method validation and machine validation from start to finish.

I’ve validated systems including MES, Polarion and a range of equipment systems.

I work to GAMP® 5 Second Edition, EU Annex 11 and 21 CFR Part 11 principles.

The documents follow your templates and procedures, and the work runs in this order.

  1. 4.1

    Plan

    A validation plan scoped to the system, its intended use and your quality system.

  2. 4.2

    Assess the risk

  3. 4.3

    Test

    IQ/OQ/PQ protocols written and executed, with results recorded as you go.

  4. 4.4

  5. 4.5

5.0 Questions about validation

5.1Who is this for?
Medical device and other regulated manufacturers working under a quality system. They need a computerised system validated, or existing validation brought up to date and kept that way.
5.2What do you deliver?
A validation plan, a risk assessment, and IQ, OQ and PQ protocols written and executed. Also a traceability matrix, a validation report, periodic review and validation of data migrations.
5.3Which guidance do you work to?
I work to GAMP® 5 Second Edition, EU Annex 11 and 21 CFR Part 11 principles.
5.4What systems have you validated?
Systems including MES, Polarion and a range of equipment systems.
5.5Can you work to our own templates?
Yes. I’m happy to work to your QA templates and procedures so the documents fit how your team already works.
5.6Can you guarantee the outcome of an audit?
No. I do the validation work to your procedures and document it so your QA team can review it.
5.7Do you work in Scotland?
Yes. I’m based in Glasgow and work with manufacturers in Scotland.
5.8How do we start?
Tell me about the system and what your QA team needs. Twenty minutes is usually enough to scope it.
