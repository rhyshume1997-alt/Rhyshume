Admin automation for engineering and trade firms

Too much of your week goes on chasing enquiries, retyping quote requests and sending the same documents again, so I set up simple automations around the tools you already use: web forms, email, spreadsheets and your CRM

What I set up

The jobs that eat your admin time

  • Enquiry and quote request intake, captured, sorted and acknowledged automatically
  • Quote, certificate and job documents generated from templates
  • Appointment and renewal reminders
  • CRM updates and simple reporting dashboards
  • Connecting systems that don't currently talk to each other

From input to output

One example process, to show how it works, this isn't a client project

InputAn enquiry email arrives with a drawing attached
→
ProcessThe details are captured in your CRM or spreadsheet and an acknowledgement goes out

Questions

Questions about automation

What kind of jobs can you automate?

Enquiry and quote request intake, quote, certificate and job documents from templates, reminders, CRM updates and simple dashboards, and connecting systems that don't currently talk to each other

Do I have to change the tools I use?

No, I set up automations around the tools you already use, like web forms, email, spreadsheets and your CRM

What if the automation gets something wrong?

Anything it can't read or isn't sure about gets flagged for you to check rather than guessed at, and documents come out as drafts for you to approve before they go anywhere

What is the pilot?

A pilot of 2 to 4 weeks on one process, so you can see if it's worth it before committing to more, with optional monthly care afterwards
