Where automation sits

The steps automation takes off your desk

These are the steps in the lifecycle that automation handles

  1. Enquiry + drawing

    Service: Website and Automation

    The old way: Enquiries arrive as emails and phone messages, drawings turn up in different places, and details get missed

    With the system: A quote request form asks what you'd ask on the phone, takes the drawing if you want it to, and sends the request straight to your email

    The outcome: No lost enquiries, and each one arrives complete

  2. Quote drafted

    Service: Automation and Tolra

    The old way: Someone retypes the enquiry into a quote document and checks it against the template by hand

    With the system: The details are captured and a quote is drafted from your template for you to check

    The outcome: Quotes go out faster, and nobody retypes the same details twice

  3. Approved & sent

    Service: Automation and Tolra

    The old way: Finished quotes wait in drafts until someone has time to check and email them

    With the system: Nothing goes out until a person approves it, and then it's sent in one step

    The outcome: You stay in control, and approved quotes don't wait around

  4. Follow-up

    Service: Automation and Tolra

    The old way: Chasing a quote depends on someone remembering to ring or email

    With the system: Follow-ups and chasing by email go out when you schedule them, and the dashboard lists quotes with no reply

    The outcome: Fewer quotes go cold, and you can see which ones still need an answer

  5. Reviewed & repeat work

    Service: Website and Automation

    The old way: Nobody asks for a review, and past customers are forgotten until they ring again

    With the system: A Google Business Profile and a review link make it easy to leave a review, and reminders can prompt follow-up with past customers

    The outcome: Reviews build trust, and repeat work comes back to a firm that's easy to find
