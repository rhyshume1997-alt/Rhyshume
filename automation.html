1.0 Scope

Enquiries get chased by hand, quote requests are retyped, and the same documents are sent again and again

It is work that a system should do, and every hour of it is an hour not spent on the job

2.0 What I build

I set up simple automations around web forms, email, spreadsheets and your CRM, so nothing needs replacing

  • Enquiry and quote request intake, captured, sorted and acknowledged automatically
  • Quote, certificate and job documents generated from templates
  • Appointment and renewal reminders
  • CRM updates and simple reporting dashboards
  • Connecting systems that don’t currently talk to each other

Enquiry and quote request intake, quote, certificate and job documents from templates, reminders, CRM updates and simple dashboards, and connecting systems that don’t currently talk to each other
