2.5 Plain automation, AI or a custom system?

AI is not magic, and it is not always the answer.

If the steps are always the same, plain automation is quicker to test and easier to trust. AI helps when the input is messy, such as reading an email and pulling out what is being asked for. Even then, a person approves the result before it goes anywhere.

If your process lives in five spreadsheets and nobody agrees which one is right, a small custom system is the better answer. Automating a mess only makes the mess faster. I will tell you which of the three I think fits, even if it is the simplest one.
