1.2Where work gets lost

These are the problems I hear about most. You may recognise some of them.

  • An RFQ arrives by email with a drawing, and the details are retyped into a quote.
  • Quotes go out and nobody chases them.
  • Job status lives in a spreadsheet, on a whiteboard and in someone’s head.
  • Inspection results, due dates and delivery details sit in different places.
  • Customers ring to ask where their order is.

1.3What exists today, and what I would build for you

Built and in use. The JNR Engineering website, where quote requests go straight to the firm’s inbox. And Tolra, my job management software, which is in pilot with a Glasgow shop. In the pilot it has a job board, a dashboard of what needs you today, follow-ups by email, revenue tracking, and AI that drafts quotes and invoices for you to approve.

Possible custom builds. These are not built yet, so I would scope and quote each one for your firm. For example: pulling the details of an emailed RFQ into your quote template, sending customers an update when a job reaches a stage, or joining your quotes up to your accounts package. I cannot promise a saving or a return, because it depends on how you work today.

How a website and the systems fit together. The website is where the enquiry starts. The quote and the job carry on from there, and the invoice follows. Each part works alone. They work better when they share the same details, so nothing is typed in twice.

2.5 What can be designed into an agreed solution

These are things I can design into a system for your firm once we have agreed what it must do. None of them is claimed as already available to you. I scope each one with you, and I tell you if I have not built it before.

Capabilities that could be part of an agreed solution
CapabilityWhat it could mean for your firm
Document revision controlsEach document has a version, a date and an author, and earlier versions are kept.
User permissionsPeople see and change only what their role allows.
Audit trailsWho changed what, and when, is recorded and can be reviewed.
ApprovalsA named person approves a quote, a document or a change before it moves on.
TraceabilityA job links back to the enquiry, the quote, the drawing revision and the records behind it.
Change recordsA change to a requirement or to the system is written down with a reason and a date.
Test evidenceWhat was tested, how and with what result is kept alongside the system.
Controlled reportingReports are drawn from the same records, so the numbers agree.
Customer and job recordsOne record for each customer and job, instead of copies in several places.
Requirements documentationA written statement of what the system must do, agreed before anything is built.

Demonstrated. The JNR Engineering website, which is live, and Tolra, my job management software, which is in pilot with a Glasgow shop. I do not claim that every item in the table above is already built into either of them.

Not claimed. I do not claim regulatory approval, certification or a validated status for any system on this site, and a system I build for you does not by itself make your business compliant with a standard or a regulation.
