1.0 What the website is for

I build the front of the system: the site that’s easy to find, the form that takes the enquiry and the drawing, and the Google profile where customers can leave reviews. This page is written for engineering and manufacturing firms. If you run another kind of small business, see websites for small businesses.

2.1Mobile-friendly sites

2.2Quote request forms

Each page ends in the next step your customers are most likely to want, such as ringing, asking for a quote or booking a time. I agree which ones with you.

I review how enquiries reach you today, send a written proposal for the work, and if you’re happy with it, I build the site.
