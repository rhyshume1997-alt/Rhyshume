Where the website sits

The website is the front of the system

These are the steps in the lifecycle that the website handles

  1. Found online

    Service: Website

    The old way: Customers find you through word of mouth or an old listing, and a site that's awkward on a phone loses the rest

    With the system: A mobile-friendly site, local search set-up and a Google Business Profile, with clear page content and structured data so search engines and AI assistants can read what you make

    The outcome: More of the right enquiries find you in the first place

  2. Enquiry + drawing

    Service: Website and Automation

    The old way: Enquiries arrive as emails and phone messages, drawings turn up in different places, and details get missed

    With the system: A quote request form asks what you'd ask on the phone, takes the drawing if you want it to, and sends the request straight to your email

    The outcome: No lost enquiries, and each one arrives complete

  3. Reviewed & repeat work

    Service: Website and Automation

    The old way: Nobody asks for a review, and past customers are forgotten until they ring again

    With the system: A Google Business Profile and a review link make it easy to leave a review, and reminders can prompt follow-up with past customers

    The outcome: Reviews build trust, and repeat work comes back to a firm that's easy to find
