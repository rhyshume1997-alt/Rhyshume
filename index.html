How the system works

From first search to repeat work

Follow one job through the system and see what changes at each step. Select a step, switch between the old way and the connected system, or press auto-play

  1. Found online

    Service: Website

    The old way: Customers find you through word of mouth or an old listing, and a site that's awkward on a phone loses the rest

    With the system: A mobile-friendly site, local search set-up and a Google Business Profile, with clear page content and structured data so search engines and AI assistants can read what you make

    The outcome: More of the right enquiries find you in the first place

  2. Enquiry + drawing

    Service: Website and Automation

    The old way: Enquiries arrive as emails and phone messages, drawings turn up in different places, and details get missed

    With the system: A quote request form asks what you'd ask on the phone, takes the drawing if you want it to, and sends the request straight to your email

    The outcome: No lost enquiries, and each one arrives complete

  3. Quote drafted

    Service: Automation and Tolra

    The old way: Someone retypes the enquiry into a quote document and checks it against the template by hand

    With the system: The details are captured and a quote is drafted from your template for you to check

    The outcome: Quotes go out faster, and nobody retypes the same details twice

  4. Approved & sent

    Service: Automation and Tolra

    The old way: Finished quotes wait in drafts until someone has time to check and email them

    With the system: Nothing goes out until a person approves it, and then it's sent in one step

    The outcome: You stay in control, and approved quotes don't wait around

  5. Follow-up

    Service: Automation and Tolra

    The old way: Chasing a quote depends on someone remembering to ring or email

    With the system: Follow-ups and chasing by email go out when you schedule them, and the dashboard lists quotes with no reply

    The outcome: Fewer quotes go cold, and you can see which ones still need an answer

  6. Order won

    Service: Tolra

    The old way: A won quote is retyped into a job sheet, and the details drift between documents

    With the system: Quotes, sales orders and jobs sit in one system, so the details are entered once

    The outcome: Fewer mistakes between the quote and the job

  7. Job scheduled

    Service: Tolra

    The old way: Jobs are planned on a whiteboard or in someone's head, and a late job is found out when the customer rings

    With the system: Every job sits on one job board with a due date and a stage, and the dashboard shows which jobs are running late

    The outcome: Late jobs are spotted early, before the customer rings

  8. Made & inspected

    Service: Tolra and CSV

    The old way: Inspection results live on paper or in a folder, and nobody can see what is waiting to be checked

    With the system: Finished work waiting to be checked is listed in one place and results are recorded against the job, and for regulated manufacturers I validate the system that holds those records

    The outcome: Fewer errors reach the customer, and the records are audit-ready

  9. Delivered

    Service: Tolra

    The old way: Nobody is sure what has gone out and what is still on the floor, so the phone rings

    With the system: Finished jobs are marked complete on the job board, so everyone can see what has shipped and what is waiting

    The outcome: Fewer calls asking where an order is

  10. Invoiced

    Service: Tolra

    The old way: Invoices are retyped from the job sheet days later, and late payment is noticed late

    With the system: AI drafts the invoice for you to approve and send, and the dashboard shows money that's late

    The outcome: Invoices go out sooner, and late payment is visible

  11. Reviewed & repeat work

    Service: Website and Automation

    The old way: Nobody asks for a review, and past customers are forgotten until they ring again

    With the system: A Google Business Profile and a review link make it easy to leave a review, and reminders can prompt follow-up with past customers

    The outcome: Reviews build trust, and repeat work comes back to a firm that's easy to find
