After school should feel like the best bit of the day. A table, some paper, a game on the go and room to be yourself.

Sunny Hours Club is a made-up club for primary-school children, designed here as an activity board. Everything on this page is sample content: the activities are ideas, and the details parents need are marked as placeholders until a real club fills them in.

The weekly planner

Build their week

Pick up an activity and put it on a day. Each day has a first and a second activity. Drag with a mouse, tap a tile then tap a slot, or use the keyboard. Nothing is booked, it is a sketch of a week.

The planner needs JavaScript. It lets you place sample activities onto Monday to Friday and see the week at a glance. Each day has the same shape:

  1. Pick-up and snack
  2. First activity
  3. Second activity
  4. Home time (times to be confirmed)

Sample activities: Make, Move, Play, Read, Grow garden and Music.
