Wedding photographer, a made-up business

Olive & Ivory

A soft, editorial site that feels like a photographer's contact sheet.

The name writes itself in handwriting beside a film window. Scrolling slides a photograph into a proof sheet, where visitors mark favourite frames Keep or Pass before they get in touch.

What it demonstrates

  • Photo gallery with a big-photo viewer
  • Favourites that carry into the enquiry
