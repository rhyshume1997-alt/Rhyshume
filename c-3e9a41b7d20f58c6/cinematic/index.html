Eight concept websites

Each one is a separate art direction with its own signature interaction and a demonstration journey. Nothing is sent, stored, booked or paid. Not merged, not deployed to production.

  • 01 Wedding photographyOlive & IvoryPen-stroke script name, a film window and a print that docks into an interactive proof sheet with a light table and Keep and Pass marks.
  • 02 Electrical servicesBright Circuit ElectricalA drawn schematic runs down the page and energises each section. Breaker switches, emergency action always in reach, a service enquiry journey.
  • 03 RestaurantSalt & EmberLetters ignite beside real fire footage, a pinned scroll hand-off into the menu, course tabs with photography.
  • 04 After school clubSunny Hours ClubA flat-shape activity board with a drag, tap or keyboard weekly planner.
  • 05 CoachingClear Ground CoachingA calm editorial page with one illustrated journey line and a reflective consultation enquiry.
  • 06 CaféLittle Kettle CaféCoffee footage in the opening, a flip-tile specials board and a tactile menu.
  • 07 Gift and home shopThe Corner ShelfDrawn products on staggered shelves, physical filtering, inspection and a demonstration basket.
  • 08 BarberOak & RazorDramatic kinetic type tied to the footage, an expanding services list and an appointment journey.
