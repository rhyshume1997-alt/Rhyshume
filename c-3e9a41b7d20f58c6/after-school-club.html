A day at the club

  1. 1

    Collected from school

    We meet your child at the gate and walk back together.

  2. 2

    Snack and a catch-up

    Something to eat, a drink, and time to talk about the day.

  3. 3

    Homework or play

    A calm corner for homework, and a space to run about when it is done.

  4. 4

    Home with you

    You collect your child, and we tell you how the afternoon went.
