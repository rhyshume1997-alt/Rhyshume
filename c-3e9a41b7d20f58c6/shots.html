Photo shot list

What each photo should show

Every grey block on the concept pages says what its photo is. Here are they all in one place, each with a prompt you can paste into ChatGPT.

Use real photos for real work.For a client who does the job, photos of their own work, premises and team will always beat generated ones, and are the only honest choice for before and afters, results, staff and food they actually serve. Use generated images only for mood and backgrounds, never to show work the business did not do or people who do not exist as its staff or customers. Check the tool's terms for commercial use.
