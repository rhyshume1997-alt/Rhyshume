The Google Ads skill, in plain words

A skill for Claude Code that checks a Google Ads account against what the business actually wants, shows where money went for nothing, scores every ad, and writes the fixes for you to run.

It never logs in to Google Ads. It holds no password, token or key, and nothing in the account changes until you press Run yourself.

How it reaches the account without a login

  • InClaude writes a read-only script. You paste it once into Google Ads (Tools, Bulk actions, Scripts). It saves one file to your Google Drive, and you put that file in gads/exports/.
  • CheckThe skill reads the file on your side and prints the check: settings, searches, keywords and ads, each with its number.
  • OutFor the options you choose, Claude writes a second script. You paste it, press Preview to see what would change, then Run.

Some fixes have no script. For those you get the click path to follow in Google Ads, one step per line.

First time: six questions

Open Claude Code in a folder for the business and say "check my Google Ads". After showing an example, it asks these once and saves the answers in gads/brief.json.

  1. What is the business, and what should the ads bring?Leads, sales on a website, calls, or visits to a place.
  2. What can one of those cost at most?If you don't know, it works it out with you: a £600 customer and 1 enquiry in 10 converting means £60 an enquiry at most.
  3. How much a month for ads?The budget the check holds the account to.
  4. Where are the customers?And whether people searching from somewhere else count. A plumber: no. A hotel: yes.
  5. What language do they search in?Checked against each campaign's language setting.
  6. Your brand name, and phone hoursSo the brand is never blocked by mistake, and the ad schedule is checked if calls matter.

  • WRONG A setting that goes against what you said you want
  • WASTE A search word with enough spent on it, and nothing back, to block
  • OFF A keyword worth pausing
  • CHECK Worth a look. It may be fine for you

  • LOW A keyword Google rates poorly
  • OK As you asked
  • ADD A search that brings results and is not a keyword yet

The routine once it is set up

  1. Say "check my Google Ads"It reads the newest export. If the file is over a week old it asks for a fresh one.
  2. Read the check and pick optionsSay which numbers you want, or none. Leaving everything alone can be the right answer.
  3. Paste the change script, Preview, then RunPreview changes nothing. The script skips anything that was renamed or changed since the check.
  4. Export again in a few daysThe next check says what was fixed and what is new. The first days after a change look odd while Google adjusts.

The export script can run itself weekly from Google Ads, so a fresh file is always waiting in Drive.

"Write me a better ad"New headlines and descriptions that have to pass the same score before you see them. It never invents a claim: unknown facts are left as [brackets] for you to fill.
"New campaign for ..."A settings sheet for the campaign, the structure, and ads. After you publish, it checks that what is live matches the plan.
"What am I paying for" or "negative keywords"The searches people really typed, sorted by what they cost and brought.
"Should I use AI Max, search partners, broad match, Performance Max"An answer for your goal, with your numbers if there is an export.
"Is my tracking right"How conversions are counted, checked first, because every other number depends on it.
"I can't run scripts"Two other ways in: a Search terms report plus screenshots, or Google's own connector.

The rules it holds itself to

  • Nothing changes unless you run itIt proposes. You are the only one who can change the account.
  • Only what you pickedA yes to one option is not a yes to another.
  • No invented numbersEvery figure comes from your export. No benchmarks, no promised results.
  • Enough data before a verdictA word is only called waste after three times your target was spent on it with nothing back. It says "too early" when it is.
  • Meaning is checked by readingBefore a word is blocked it is read against what you sell, so a real customer is not shut out.
  • Your claims are yoursYears, reviews, prices and guarantees only go in an ad if you said they are true.

What it covers

  • Search campaigns, in full: networks, locations, languages, AI Max, bidding, budgets, auto-apply, conversion setup, search terms, keywords and every responsive search ad.
  • Performance Max, in part: location, bidding, final URL expansion, brand exclusions and asset strength.
  • Display, Video, Demand Gen, Shopping and App: location and bidding only.

It cannot see your landing page, call recordings, lead quality, or anything after the click that is not a conversion in Google Ads. Your own sales records stay the truth for money.

Needs Claude Code and Node.js 18 or newer. Each business keeps its own gads/ folder, so open Claude Code in the same folder each time.
