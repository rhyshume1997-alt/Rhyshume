About Marpet

Distance is no issue.

Marpet Fabrications was established in the early 1980s in Glasgow and has built a reputation for quality with competitiveness across the sheet metal, engineering and fabrication industry. That has given us a large client base across Scotland and the North of England, in the food and drinks sector in particular.

We are east of Glasgow, ideally placed for local customers and for those further afield by the M74 road network.
