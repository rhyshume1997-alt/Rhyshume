## What "optimised" means here

- The supplied files were already small (about 0.2 megapixels each, 27 to 70 KB). The work was format conversion (WebP with a JPEG fallback), not size reduction.
- **Nothing is upscaled.** The earlier `@2x.webp` enlargements were removed in Rev 3. Every image is shown at or below its native pixel size on the screens it was designed for, and none is described as a high resolution original. They are low resolution generated concept images and must be replaced before any real use.

## Resolution is the main constraint

- The images are about 360 x 540 px. A full width landscape hero at 1280 px wide would enlarge them more than 3x and look soft.
- The proofs are therefore built around **portrait plates** at modest sizes (about 500 to 650 px tall at 1x), shown near native size and never enlarged to fill a wide frame.
- Production images should be **at least 2400 px on the long edge**, ideally 3200 px, so the same layouts stay sharp on large and high density screens.

## Rules

- Generated images appear only on fictional concept pages marked "Concept by Rhys Hume, not a live site."
- For real client work use the client's own photographs, with permission, and record filename, purpose, source, licence and aspect ratio here.
