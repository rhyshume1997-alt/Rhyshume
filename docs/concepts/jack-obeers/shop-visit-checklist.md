# Jack O'Beers & Deli: shop visit checklist

13 Station Road, Milngavie, G62 8PG. Open Wednesday to Monday, 11am to 7pm (Tuesday not confirmed). Tick as you go. Everything marked **SLOT** replaces a placeholder on the site, so name files exactly as shown.

- [ ] Phone or camera charged, spare battery, enough storage (shoot 4K video, RAW or highest-quality JPEG)
- [ ] Tripod or a stable surface, a small clip-on light or a white card for bounce, microfibre cloth, lens wipe
- [ ] Notebook, or voice memo on, for answers (section 5)
- [ ] The preview link open on your phone to show them: https://rhyshume-f1m7j2wza-rhsys-projects.vercel.app/p-5c2e81a7f3b04d96/site/ (share link, expires 1:42 PM on 25 Nov; ask me for a fresh one)
- [ ] Printed or on-screen: the logo animation brief (`logo-animation-spec.md`) and the product list template (section 4)
- [ ] Ask them first: who may appear on camera, and get a quick written "OK to use in the website" from anyone filmed or photographed

## 2. Photograph
### 2a. The scroll backgrounds (most important, needs the tripod)
**Beers page: "pint empties as you scroll"**: six photos of the **same pint glass** in the **same spot**, nothing moved between frames.
- [ ] Tripod, **locked focus, locked exposure, locked white balance**, 3:2 landscape, at least 1920 px wide (ideally 4000 px)
- [ ] Same glass, same bar or table, same background (dim warm bar backdrop works well), light from the same side
- [ ] Take **6 frames**: `beer-1` full with foam head, `beer-2` about 80%, `beer-3` about 60%, `beer-4` about 40%, `beer-5` about 20%, `beer-6` empty with foam lacing on the glass. Sip or pour between frames, don't touch the camera.
- [ ] Take a few extras of each stage (they can pick the best)
- [ ] Wipe fingerprints and condensation as needed, keep some condensation, it looks great

**Keezer rental: "glass fills as you scroll"**: same method, the keezer with taps and a glass underneath.
- [ ] `keezer-1` empty glass under the tap, then 5 stages to `keezer-6` full pint with head. Taps, wood panel and drip tray identical in all six.
- [ ] Also ask: what exactly is the keezer (chest freezer converted to taps?), how many taps, which kegs fit

### 2b. Products (every item they will sell online)
- [ ] **Each bottle, can, keg, wine, cider**: front-on on a plain surface, then one angled shot. White or light grey card behind, even light, no glare on glass
- [ ] **Label close-up** of each (for allergens/ABV, ingredients), so you can read it later
- [ ] **Deli items**: cheese wedges, pies, charcuterie, chutneys, sweet things, each on a board, plus a flat-lay of the counter
- [ ] **Gift hampers**: every hamper, lid open, overhead, plus closed and ribboned, and a close-up of the contents
- [ ] **Lifestyle**: a glass and a pie on a table, hands holding a pint (adults only), a hamper on a counter. No children in any photo that includes alcohol.
- [ ] File names: `product-name-1.jpg`, `product-name-2.jpg` (the shop can match them to Shopify)

### 2c. The shop and the people
- [ ] Exterior: the whole front, the sign close-up, the door, the street (daylight, then dusk if open late)
- [ ] Interior wide shots from each corner, the counter, the shelves, the taps, the fridges
- [ ] Details: handwritten menu boards, price tickets, chalkboards, the till area, packaging, bags, labels, stickers
- [ ] **Team portraits**: only people who agree, plain wall, natural light, 2 shots each, plus a candid at the counter
- [ ] Anything that shows the **brand colours** in the real world: signage paint, shirts, bags, awning, door (hold a colour card next to it if you have one)

### 2d. Design references to collect
- [ ] The **logo in any form**: a vector file (SVG, AI, PDF) is the priority, the current PNG is only 355 px. Ask who made it.
- [ ] **Exact brand colours**: hex codes or Pantone, or photograph the logo on a printed item
- [ ] **Fonts used** on their signs, menus and packaging (the site uses Source Sans 3 as a stand-in for Dubai, licence for Dubai not confirmed)
- [ ] Existing **menus, price lists, leaflets, posters, stickers**: photograph flat, or ask for PDFs
- [ ] **Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google Business** links, and any existing photos/videos they own (ask for the originals)
- [ ] What they like and dislike on other websites (two or three examples)

## 3. Design decisions to settle with them (show the preview on your phone)
- [ ] Do they like **Jack headbutting the page** between pages? Every link, or only some? Do they have more mascot artwork or ideas for him?
- [ ] "Craft beer" or "British beer" in the wording? Their tagline is "Fresh from keg to bottle, no foam, no fuss." Keep it?
- [ ] Colours and fonts: happy with dark mahogany and forest green with cream and amber, or does the logo suggest different ones?
- [ ] The **pint-drain** and **keezer-fill** scroll backgrounds: keep? Put the keezer one on the Kegs page? (I have not changed the Kegs page without their say-so.)
- [ ] Do they want the keezer **rental** on its own page, and should it be bookable online or enquiry only?
- [ ] Gift hampers: fixed set, build-your-own, gift messages, send to a different address?
- [ ] Newsletter provider and what they would send (events, new arrivals, offers)

## 4. Information to collect (copy straight into a table, one row per product)
For **each product**: name, short description, style or type, **ABV**, size, **price**, stock, **allergens** (from the label), tasting notes, "goes well with", producer/brewery, photo file names, which category (beer, cider, wine, keg, hamper, deli).
Do **not** guess ABV or allergens; take them from the label or the supplier sheet.

## 5. Questions to ask (and write the answer next to each)
**The business**
- [ ] Trading name: "Jack O'Beers" or "Jack O' Beers & Deli"? (the email signature says "Jack O' Beers & Deli")
- [ ] Opening hours, **including Tuesday** (we only know Wednesday to Monday, 11am to 7pm), bank holidays, Christmas
- [ ] Phone and email to publish (we have 07405 480950 and sales@jackobeers.com)
- [ ] Their story, how long open, who is behind it, what makes them different, what they do **not** do
- [ ] Event enquiries: what events, capacity, prices, who handles them

**Selling online (Shopify)**
- [ ] Is the Shopify store live? Which plan? Can they add a **Storefront API** token (steps are in the README), or set up with you present? **Never share passwords by message.**
- [ ] Do the till and Shopify stock stay in sync? Which till? (the workbook says DNA)
- [ ] Which payment methods are on? Are Apple Pay and Google Pay enabled?
- [ ] Taxes, VAT number display, and which products are age-restricted

**Click & collect**
- [ ] Days, time windows, lead time (we assume Wednesday to Monday, 11am to 7pm in three windows), how many orders per slot, who may collect, ID check at collection, what happens if an item is out of stock

**Shipping and alcohol (licensing, handle carefully)**
- [ ] Their **premises licence**: does it cover **off-sales** (they said yes), delivery, and distance sales? Ask to see the **operating plan** and the licence conditions
- [ ] Which courier, which countries (UK only, or international "where possible"), costs and times, packaging for glass, **age check on delivery** and who does it
- [ ] Minimum order, free delivery threshold, returns and damaged goods policy, refunds on alcohol
- [ ] Age verification at checkout: a self-declaration tick only, or something stronger?

**Keezer / kegs**
- [ ] What it is exactly, how many kegs per unit, what they rent (keezer, kegs, gas, glasses?), price, deposit, dates, delivery and collection, who sets up, insurance, damage rules, minimum age of hirer

**Marketing and legal**
- [ ] Do they want to show customers or staff? Who signs the photo permission?
- [ ] No content aimed at children. Confirm the audience is adults.
- [ ] Privacy notice, terms of sale and returns text: who will check them (a solicitor or adviser)?
- [ ] Domain: is it jackobeers.com, who controls DNS, and who hosts email?

## 6. Before you leave
- [ ] Back up everything twice (phone + laptop or cloud) before leaving the shop
- [ ] Write the file list: which photos fill which **SLOT** (hero, beer 1 to 6, keezer 1 to 6, each product, deli, hampers, shop, team)
- [ ] Agree what you will send them next and when (preview link, then the quote/spec stays separate)
- [ ] Note anything they asked you to change in the design, in their words

## 7. What happens next (so you can tell them)
1. Photos and video go into the site under the file names above, replacing the temporary AI images.
2. Their Shopify products load into the shelves automatically once the store address and token are added (one file, `config.js`).
3. Placeholder links, address details and "to be confirmed" tags get replaced with the real answers.
4. Logo animation clips (the lying-down and headbutt moves) replace the stand-in; the brief is `logo-animation-spec.md`.
5. A compliance read-through of age checks, delivery and the legal pages before anything goes live.
