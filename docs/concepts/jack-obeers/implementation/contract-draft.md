## 2. The services

The Supplier will design and build a custom online storefront for the Client. It is built in Next.js and connects to the Client's existing Shopify store through the Shopify Storefront API. It is a "headless" shop.

Shopify stays responsible for products, stock, cart, orders and checkout. The website does not hold its own product database, stock records or payment system. Customers pay on Shopify's hosted checkout.

Included, as set out in the Statement of Requirements (Rev [to fill], to be signed with this contract):

1. Homepage, including the logo animation: the lying-down logo stands up and leads into the shop
2. Beer, cider and wine catalogue and product pages. Each shows a description, flavour profile, food pairings and allergens
3. Deli catalogue and product pages
4. Gift hampers page
5. Cart and Shopify hosted checkout
6. Click and collect, using Shopify local pickup
7. Delivery information page
8. About page
9. Contact page and event enquiry form
10. Customer accounts, using Shopify's customer accounts (login hosted by Shopify)
11. Design using the Client's logo colours, rounded and soft-edged shapes, and the Client's real photographs and video. Placeholders are used only where an image is missing.
12. Connection of the finished site to the Client's domain, once the Client approves

## 8. Alcohol sales, licensing and payment timing **[Solicitor]**

1. The Client is responsible for holding and complying with every licence needed to sell alcohol, including online, click and collect and delivery sales, and for confirming this with the local licensing authority. The Supplier adds age verification wording and checkout prompts as instructed by the Client. The Supplier does not give legal or licensing advice and does not accept responsibility for licensing compliance.
2. The Client has told the Supplier that the premises licence can cover off-sales, but that payment for alcohol may only be authorised between 10:00 and 22:00. The Client will give the Supplier the licence and operating plan details to check before alcohol ordering is switched on. [to fill: confirm exact hours and what the licence and operating plan say]
3. Until those details are verified and the Client approves in writing, alcohol checkout and alcohol delivery are switched off. Everything else in the shop can go live.
4. Once approved, orders containing alcohol placed outside 10:00 to 22:00 are held, and payment is authorised only from 10:00 the next morning. This depends on what Shopify and the Client's payment settings can do. The Supplier will test this and report what works. The Supplier will not change Shopify payment settings without the Client's written approval, and this clause does not mean the arrangement satisfies Scottish licensing law. The Client should confirm that.
5. The following will be agreed in writing before alcohol orders are switched on: age checks at order, collection and delivery; courier checks at handover; minimum unit pricing on every alcohol line; records of deliveries; and where deliveries may go.
6. International shipping is not included. It may be available later, subject to licence checks.
