# Ask him and get: 10 things

Never ask for passwords, card details or private keys. Access comes by invite.

## Ask him

- [ ] 1. Is the grant approved yet? When will he hear?
- [ ] 2. Is he happy with the custom storefront? (It needs a new quote. The £2,000 stays until he agrees.)
- [ ] 3. Pay in full or monthly?
- [ ] 4. Does he want customer accounts at launch?
- [ ] 5. Does his premises licence cover selling to take away, online and delivery? What hours?
- [ ] 6. Who checks age at collection and delivery? Alcohol delivery at launch, or collection only?
- [ ] 7. Does the DNA till stock sync with Shopify?

## Get from him

- [ ] 8. Shopify collaborator code (4 digits) and store address.
- [ ] 9. Where is his domain (GoDaddy?) and does it show his Shopify shop today?
- [ ] 10. The logo as a vector file, the product list with allergens, and a shoot day.

Say before you leave: nothing starts until the grant is approved and you have both signed. Alcohol ordering stays off until his licence checks are done. You will send a revised quote and the contract.
