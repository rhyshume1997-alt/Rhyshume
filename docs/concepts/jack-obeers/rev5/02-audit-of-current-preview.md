# Rev 5: audit of the current preview against the workbook

Inspected: the live preview (`site/`), full-page screenshots at 1366 and 390, and the workbook.

**Represented:** warm and rounded styling; product descriptors, flavour dials, pairings, allergens (placeholder); click and collect, shipping, hampers and members pages; logo animation as a stand-in; pour footage; two-click reach to products.

**Missing:** the **brown beer bottle cursor** (a recorded request) is not on the current site at all; the logo "gets up and becomes the new page" is only a footer gag; no real "New York deli" material (tiles, awning, stubs); Dubai-like type is only a stand-in.

**Interpreted wrongly:** the logo sits on a hard square plate, which is "blocky" (the workbook says not blocky, rounded, faded edges); how-it-works illustrations are flat generic icons; the pour is a small clip behind a headline rather than the identity of the page.

**No clear tie to the brief:** the forest-green card tiles, the six-frame pint background (good idea, but disconnected from the opening) and the AI keezer page were my additions, not client requests.

**Generic or unfinished:** the home page is a stack of equal cards (three tiles, four cards, three tiles); every section has the same rhythm; headlines are modest; section transitions are a thin wood strip; photo slots are empty beige boxes.

**Too cartoonish:** the how-it-works icons, the bull sign pasted on a box.

**Unnecessarily dark:** the shelf pages that use the pint background hide product cards on a dark panel; the hero is dark for the whole first screen with little warmth.

**Not welcoming:** no human cue in the first screen (no hours or "come in"); the opening is a headline plus a small sign.

**Harder shopping:** the shop route is a mega-menu to a page of identical cards; no quick "what do you fancy" route; flavour info is only on the product page.

## What the original eight concepts do differently (extracted principles)
1. **Type is the architecture:** headlines run 6 to 14 rem, mixing roman and italic (Salt & Ember, Little Kettle) or solid and outline (Corner Shelf). The headline is half the hero.
2. **Photography is cropped by a shape:** arches and circles (Little Kettle, Sunny Hours), stamps and stickers overlap the edge. Never a plain rectangle.
3. **Full-bleed colour blocks:** each section is one colour edge to edge with a deliberate edge (scallop, zigzag), not cards on a single background.
4. **One signature interaction per concept, from a real object** (course list that swaps the picture, flip tiles, ticket stubs, shelf with objects).
5. **Index lists as navigation:** large numbered rows (01 Kitchen, arrow) make the two-click route obvious and beautiful.
6. **Honest tags** on generated media; mobile re-composed, not just stacked.
Benchmark ambition (Tobia Donadon): confident scale, one idea per screen, motion that explains. No assets or layouts copied.
