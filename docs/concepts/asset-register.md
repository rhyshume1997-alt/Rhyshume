# Asset register: eight cinematic concept websites (Rev 6)

All media here is **generated fictional concept material**. None of it shows a real business, customers or completed client work, and every page says so on screen. The generating tools' commercial-use terms have not been checked, so licence status is "unverified, concept use only". Do not publish any of it outside these hidden, noindex concept pages without separate approval.

Source: `RDH Eight Website Concepts Handoff.zip` supplied by Rhys (hero stills, hero clips, supporting photographs).

## Hero clips and posters (one per concept)

Every supplied clip is **736 x 400 px, landscape, 24 fps, 5 to 6 seconds, no audio**. The clips are used landscape and **never stretched into portrait**. They are shown no larger than a screenshot at 1440 and 1920 px proved acceptable (see the review). They are **not upscaled in the files**: the browser scales them on screen, which is why large or detailed areas look soft. Each page's design treatment is chosen to suit that softness.

## Remaining gaps

- **Footage:** all eight clips are 736 x 400. They look soft when shown large. Higher resolution masters (at least 1920 x 1080, ideally 2560 x 1440 or 3840 x 2160), and separate portrait phone compositions, are needed before any of these go beyond concept review.
- **Olive & Ivory:** nine of the planned twelve sheet frames exist, all low resolution. Three more are needed (the manifest in `asset-manifest.md` lists them) and every existing frame needs a high resolution original.
- **Salt & Ember:** four menu photographs, all low resolution.
- **Other six concepts:** no supporting photographs were supplied. Pages use stills taken from their own clip at native size (labelled generated concept images) and original drawn SVG and CSS illustration. Product photography for The Corner Shelf, food and drink photography for Little Kettle Café, and activity photography for Sunny Hours Club would be needed for any real use.
- **Real information:** no prices, opening hours, addresses, phone numbers, staff, credentials, reviews or availability exist. Every page uses visible placeholders such as "[price to be confirmed]".
- **Licences:** commercial-use terms of the generating tools have not been checked.
