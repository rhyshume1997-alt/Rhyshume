How to run a client meeting with the workbook

For Friday 20 November 2026. One page.

Before (10 minutes)

  1. Open the workbook from the pack folder (w-82503121e7fdf54e/index.html). Always open it the same way, so your work is in the same place.
  2. Do one practice run with a made-up client. When you click START CLIENT MEETING and type a name, say OK to "practice". Practice clients are labelled.
  3. Check the Open the working demo links on the gallery. If a demo does not open, the c-3e9a41b7d20f58c6 folder is not beside the workbook folder.
  4. Check you can Save backup and find the file afterwards.

During (30 to 40 minutes, six stages)

Sit beside the client with the laptop between you. Click Hide my prompts so they only see their questions. Show them again when you need the help text.

  1. Your business. Write their words, not yours. Ask for the website address if they have one.
  2. What should it do? They tap the pictures. More than one is fine. Then ask which matters most.
  3. What should it look like? Show the eight made-up designs. They tap LOVE THIS, LIKE PARTS OF IT or NOT FOR ME, then tell you why. They do not choose a template. We build something original. Ask for websites they already like and three words for the feel.
  4. Photos and branding. What they have, what is missing, who provides it. Anything they show you is only previewed. The saved project keeps a small preview and the file name, not the original.
  5. What does it need? Everything ticked is a request. Nothing goes into a price from here.
  6. Review. Read the summary aloud. Ask "Have I understood?" Fix anything, then Finish and save.

If they do not know, tap I'm not sure yet. Nothing is made up for them.

After

  • Save backup (a file). Put it somewhere you trust.
  • Client summary (print or save as PDF) has no prices and none of your notes. Send it only when you have checked it.
  • Internal record has your notes and the draft design brief in three parts: confirmed requirements, my creative proposals, not yet confirmed.
  • Update the rest of the workbook passes answers to Questions to ask, Sites they like, Look and feel, What is in and the open questions list. It never overwrites what you typed yourself.
  • The meeting is discovery only. Scope, responsibilities, quote, statement of requirements, contract, written acceptance and deposit still follow. No price, date or discount is set by this tool.

If the internet or a preview is not available

Everything in the meeting works with no internet. The eight design demos open from the pack folder ("on this computer"). If one does not open, the screenshots on the card still show the design. The "Online copy" link needs the internet and is public, so the client never needs a login.

How safe is it?

  • Nothing is sent anywhere. Answers stay in this browser and in the backup files you save.
  • They are not encrypted and have no password. Anyone who can use the computer, the browser or the backup files can read them. Keep the laptop locked and keep backups somewhere private.
  • Presentation mode only hides things on screen. It is not security.
  • The hosted workbook's email sign-in link protects clients saved online. "Work offline" does not use it.
  • Never type passwords or account logins into the workbook.

Things to remember

  • Not VAT registered goes on quotes, with no VAT line. No discount is applied automatically. The Tolra linked website discount needs its basis confirmed first.
  • The concept photos and films are generated and fictional. Never present them as the client's work.
  • Do not store real client records in the website repository.
