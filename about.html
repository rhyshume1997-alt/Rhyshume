About Rhys Hume

Websites, automation and validation work for engineering firms

I'm Rhys Hume, based in Glasgow, my background is in computer system validation engineering at Terumo Aortic, and now I build websites and systems for engineering firms

I got into validation after studying chemistry at uni, then went on to work with Thermo Fisher and into the labs, where I moved up through method development and QC and QA work

That led to method validation and machine validation, which I ran start to finish myself for the company, improving the process and saving money along the way

Then I moved to CSV at Terumo Aortic, where the work is protocols, data migration, project work and periodic reviews, advising and helping where I'm needed, building Power BI reports, implementing AI agents and helping other departments use AI for the business

Now I build for engineering firms, I built the JNR Engineering website and the systems behind it, and Tolra, my job management app for CNC shops, is in pilot in two working shops
