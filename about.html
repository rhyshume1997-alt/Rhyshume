1.0 Who you would be dealing with

I’m Rhys Hume, based in Glasgow. I build websites, custom business systems and automations for engineering firms and other small businesses. There is no account manager and no hand-off. You talk to me, and I build it.

I come from computer system validation for regulated manufacturers, and I still take on validation work. That means proving that a computer system does what it should, with the testing and documents to show it. It is careful, practical work, and it shapes how I build for you. I ask what could go wrong before anything is built, I test with you on real examples, and I put things in writing.

I got there by studying chemistry, then working in labs and moving through method validation into computer system validation. Alongside that I have built Power BI reports and AI agents, and helped teams use AI.

Now I build for small firms. I built the JNR Engineering website and the systems behind it, and Tolra, my job management app for CNC shops, which is in pilot.

2.0 How I work

  • I look at how your work runs today, from the first enquiry to the invoice, before I suggest anything.
  • I put what I’ll build in writing, so you know what you are getting before it is built.
  • I test it with you on your own examples, and a person approves anything the system drafts before it goes out.
  • I hand it over with a walkthrough, and I stay accountable from the first chat to handover.

2.5 Why my background matters to you

I have worked with complex workflows and with computer system validation in regulated medical device settings. It is the reason I build the way I do.

  • I ask what could go wrong before anything is built, and I write down what the system must do.
  • I test with your own examples, and I keep a record of what was tested.
  • I put access, approvals and changes in the design from the start, rather than adding them later.
  • I am honest about what I have built and what I have not.

I am familiar with the quality management principles behind ISO 9000, ISO 9001 and ISO 13485. That is familiarity, not certification. My business is not ISO certified, and building a system for you does not by itself make your business compliant with a standard or regulation. I do not name past employers or discuss confidential systems.
