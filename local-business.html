6.0 Questions

6.1Can you get my business to the top of Google?
No one can guarantee rankings in Google or AI tools. What I can do is make sure your information is complete, accurate and easy to find, which is the part you control.
6.2What is the difference between a website and a website with a quote workflow?
A standard website presents your business and lets customers get in touch. The quote workflow version also drafts quotes from your template and schedules follow-ups, so there is less retyping and chasing. You check and approve each quote before it goes out.
6.3Can you help with a website I already have?
Yes. Put your address into the upgrade page and I will build a concept of an improved version, using only what is already on your site. If you like where it is going, we talk about building it properly and I send a written quote.
