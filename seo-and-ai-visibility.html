Be easier to find in search and in AI tools, with work you can see

Search engines and AI tools can only work with what your website and your business details give them. I help you get those foundations right, and I show you what I found and what I changed. I do not guarantee rankings, and I do not guarantee that an AI tool will recommend you.

1.0 What this is, and what it is not

You pay for defined professional work and for evidence of what was done. You do not pay for a promise about where you will appear.

  • A technically sound website, useful pages and consistent business information are the foundations. Search engines and AI tools both read them.
  • Nobody can guarantee a ranking, a place on a map, or a mention in an AI answer, and I do not.
  • I do not invent visibility scores. If something cannot be tested properly, I say so.
  • Ongoing work is optional and agreed in writing.

3.0 What I can look at

These are areas I can work in. I agree the list with you before anything starts, and I tell you where something is outside what I can measure.

  • Technical SEO: HTTPS, mobile layout, page loading, titles, descriptions, headings, canonical addresses, sitemap, robots rules and links that do not work.
  • Page optimisation: whether each service you sell has a clear page that says what you do and where.
  • Local search: your Google Business Profile, categories, service area and the consistency of your name, address and phone number across listings.
  • Search performance measurement, using your own Search Console data if you choose to share it.
  • AI search visibility testing, described below.
  • Reporting and prioritised recommendations, written in plain English.

4.0 How AI search visibility testing works

I write a set of questions a customer might ask, for your business and your area. For example: which companies near Glasgow can machine precision parts to a drawing? I then test them on AI tools whose terms allow it, more than once, because answers change from one run to the next.

For every run I record the exact question, the tool, the date and time, the answer, whether your business was named, whether others were named and whether sources were given. You see that record. You do not see a single score, because one number would hide how much the answers vary.

A plain chatbot reply is not the same as a live web search. Where there is no supported way to test a tool, I mark it as awaiting a supported test and I do not guess.

5.0 What you will see from me

  • Findings that say what was checked, when, how and what the limits were.
  • A clear line between a measured fact, a reasonable inference and something I could not test.
  • Before and after observations for work I carried out.
  • No claims about extra enquiries or revenue unless you have the figures and we agree they show it.

6.0 What I do not promise

  • A position in Google, on a map or in any AI answer.
  • A set number of enquiries, visitors or sales.
  • That a competitor who scores higher on a technical test is winning more work. A test result is a measurement of a website, not of a business.
  • That every item on this page applies to your business.

7.0 Questions

7.1Can you guarantee that my business will rank first in Google?
No. Nobody outside Google controls its results, and I do not promise rankings. I work on the parts you control: a technically sound site, useful pages and business details that are consistent wherever they appear.
7.2Can you make ChatGPT, Google AI or another AI tool recommend my business?
No. AI search results vary between tools, between days and between the way a question is asked. I can test how a set of customer style questions is answered and show you the record, but I cannot promise to be named.
7.3Do I need a monthly plan?
No. An assessment and the changes that come from it can be one-off pieces of work. Ongoing management is optional, agreed in writing and can be stopped.
7.4How much does it cost?
I quote for the work once I know the site, the area and the market. Use Request a quote, or discuss your requirements in a free chat. I do not publish package prices for this work.
7.5What is a business review, and how is it different from the work on this page?
A business review is a first look by me at how you are found online and how you work, which you ask for through the enquiry form. The work on this page is what follows if you want help acting on it.
7.6Do you need access to my Google accounts?
Only if you want me to work from your own data, such as Search Console or your Google Business Profile. Access is agreed first, kept to what the task needs and can be removed when the work ends.
