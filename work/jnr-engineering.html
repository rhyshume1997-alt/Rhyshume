1.0 The site today

2.0 The business problem

JNR Engineering does milling, turning, water jet and fabrication in its own workshop in Hamilton. The firm needed customers to be able to find it and get in touch easily, and it needed a better way to keep its jobs and quotes in one place. JNR is my family’s firm.

3.0 What I built

3.1The website
A clear “get a quote” button and a phone number for urgent jobs at the top of the home page.
3.2Quote requests
A quote request form that sends each request straight to the firm’s email.
3.3Local search
SEO and a Google Business Profile.
3.4Tolra
Tolra, my job management app for CNC shops, for jobs and quotes. It’s used in the workshop and in the office, and it is still in pilot. See Tolra.

Related:What to put on an engineering firm’s website, which uses this site as its example.

4.0 How it works

A visitor arrives on the home page and sees a “get a quote” button and a phone number for urgent jobs without scrolling. The quote request form sends what they type to the firm’s email. The Google Business Profile and the search set up are there so people looking for the firm see accurate details. Behind the scenes, the firm’s jobs and quotes are kept in Tolra.

5.0 What the firm can do with it

  • Receive quote requests by email at any hour.
  • Give customers a number to ring for urgent jobs.
  • Show accurate details when people search for the firm on Google.
  • Keep jobs and quotes in one place, used in the workshop and in the office.

6.0 The evidence

You can see the live site at jnrengineering.co.uk and the screenshot above. I have not measured results such as enquiries, traffic or time saved, so I do not quote any. Tolra is in pilot, so it is not yet a finished product.
