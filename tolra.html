2.0 What it does

One system for the work, from quote to invoice, used on the workshop floor and in the office, with AI built into the app

  • AI that reads from emails
  • AI drafts quotes and invoices, and nothing is sent until you approve it
  • Revenue measuring
  • Follow-ups and chasing by email, when you schedule them
  • A dashboard of what needs you today, and a job board for every job

I built Tolra myself, coming from a background in validated manufacturing systems, and I’m developing it alongside the shops using it

3.0 The steps Tolra runs

From the drafted quote to the invoice, in order

RefStepIn Tolra
3.1Quote draftedThe details are captured and a quote is drafted from your template for you to check
3.2Approved and sentNothing goes out until a person approves it, and then it’s sent in one step
3.3Follow-upFollow-ups and chasing by email go out when you schedule them, and the dashboard lists quotes with no reply
3.4Order wonQuotes, sales orders and jobs sit in one system, so the details are entered once
3.5Job scheduledEvery job sits on one job board with a due date and a stage, and the dashboard shows which jobs are running late
3.6Made and inspectedFinished work waiting to be checked is listed in one place and results are recorded against the job
3.7DeliveredFinished jobs are marked complete on the job board, so everyone can see what has shipped and what is waiting
3.8InvoicedAI drafts the invoice for you to approve and send, and the dashboard shows money that’s late
