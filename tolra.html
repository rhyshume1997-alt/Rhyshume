Questions

Questions about Tolra

What is Tolra?

Job management for small CNC and fabrication shops, built to keep track of quotes, jobs and what's waiting to be checked

Who is it for?

CNC and fabrication shops with 5 to 100 people

Is it ready to use?

It's live in two working shops, JNR Engineering and Marpet Fabrications, and still in pilot with local shops, so I'm adding features as I work with the shops involved

Does it use AI?

Yes, there's live automation built into the app that reads from emails

Can I see it?

Yes, ask about the pilot and tell me how you run jobs today, then I'll show you

Are the screenshots real?

They show demo data, not a customer's real jobs
