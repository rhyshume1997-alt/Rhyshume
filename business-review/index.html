What the review does and does not do

What it checks

  • Public pages of your website, read once with a small limit on how many.
  • A mobile speed test of your home page, run the same way for any business you name.
  • Basics such as secure connection, page titles, descriptions, headings and a sitemap.
  • How a task you find slow could become a workflow, as an idea only.

What it does not do

  • It does not measure Google Maps rankings or reviews. Those show as not tested.
  • It does not show AI search results unless a supported test has been run. Until then it says so.
  • It does not promise rankings, enquiries or returns, and it is not a quote.
  • It does not send anything to the business you name.

Every result carries a source, a date and one of six evidence labels, so you can see what was measured and what was not. The tool that reads websites is described on the crawler information page.
