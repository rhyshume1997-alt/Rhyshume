How the review reads a website

If you run a website and saw RhysHumeReviewBot in your logs, this page explains what it is and how to stop it.

What it is

RhysHumeReviewBot is the tool behind the free business opportunity review. It runs only when someone types a website address into that form. It never browses on its own and it does not build a list of sites.

What it does

  • It identifies itself as RhysHumeReviewBot/1.0 in the User-Agent header.
  • It reads robots.txt first and honours it. If a page is disallowed it is not read, and the review records that as not checked.
  • It reads a small number of public pages from the address entered, up to 25 requests in total for one review, one after another.
  • It reads only public pages over http or https. It does not log in, submit forms or run scripts on your site.
  • It looks at things such as the page title, headings, whether the site uses HTTPS, whether a sitemap exists and whether contact details are easy to find.

How to stop it

Add this to your robots.txt and the review will not read your site:

User-agent: RhysHumeReviewBot
Disallow: /

Problems or questions: rhys@rhyshume.co.uk. Please include the address of your site and the time you saw the request. A fixed list of server addresses is not published.
