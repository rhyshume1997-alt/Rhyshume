3.0 What you get

  • A fixed quote on headed paper, matched to the scheme’s eligible items, so the council can see exactly what’s being bought
  • A short digital-impact statement you can attach to your application, saying in plain terms what the project changes
  • Delivery timed to the claim deadline, so the work is finished and invoiced when the scheme needs it

4.0 Typical projects

  • A website that says what you make, what you can machine or fabricate, and how to get in touch
  • E-commerce or a quote request form that collects the job details you need before you price a job
  • One automation, like a CRM set up around your enquiries or an AI tool that takes a repetitive office job off your plate
