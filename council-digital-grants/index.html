3.0 What you get

  • A fixed quote on headed paper, matched to the scheme’s eligible items, so the council can see exactly what’s being bought.
  • A short digital-impact statement you can attach to your application, saying in plain terms what the project changes.
  • Delivery planned around the claim deadline.

4.0 Set up costs that may be eligible

  • A website that says what you make, what you can machine or fabricate, and how to get in touch.
  • Google search set up: I set up and tidy your Google Business Profile so customers can find accurate details.
  • Domain and email set up, with the domain registered in your own name.

Check the scheme guidance, because your council decides what is eligible.
