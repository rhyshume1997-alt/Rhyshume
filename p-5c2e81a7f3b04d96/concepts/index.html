Jack O’Beers & Deli: five website concepts

Earlier round The preview site

Which feels like you?

Five takes on the website you have already seen, in the same colours and the same feel. Each one has its own special idea. Have a play, then tell us what you like and don’t like.

  1. Open a concept and have a play (try the special idea).
  2. Press Review and give feedback. Tap Love it, Not sure or Don’t like for each part.
  3. Press Download my feedback.
  4. Email the file back to us.

Good to know: these are ideas for discussion. Products and prices are samples, pictures marked “temporary” are not the shop’s, and nothing here is live or final.

  • Concept 1

    The Long Pour

    The cinematic preview, taken to the whole page: the pint behind every section empties as you scroll. Special idea: the glass empties as you scroll, and you can fill it up again at the end.

    Try it: scroll slowly, then press Fill it up again.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 2

    Wake Jack

    A quiet dark counter with Jack asleep on it. Press the button and he gets up and headbutts the page out of the way. Special idea: wake Jack and every section change gets a headbutt.

    Try it: press Wake Jack.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 3

    The Shelves

    Warm, cream and shelf-first: long shelves of round cards and ticket-stub deli cards. Special idea: four taste dials (sweet, bitter, fruity, body) re-order the shelves to match what you like.

    Try it: move the dials in Find your drink.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 4

    Keg to Bottle

    Your tagline as a scroll story: a pipe runs down the page from keg to tap to bottle to hamper to the door. Special idea: the beer line fills with amber as you scroll.

    Try it: scroll down the page slowly.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 5

    The Hamper Table

    Gift-led: an open hamper on the dark counter, with beer, deli and gifts below. Special idea: pack a hamper, write the gift tag, wrap it and add it to the basket.

    Try it: pack a hamper at the top.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
