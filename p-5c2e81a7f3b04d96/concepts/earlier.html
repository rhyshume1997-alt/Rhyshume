Earlier round (different colours)

Five different ideas for the website. Each one is a working page with its own look, its own special idea and sample products. Have a play, then tell us what you like and don’t like.

  1. Open a concept and have a play (try the special idea).
  2. Press Review and give feedback. Tap Love it, Not sure or Don’t like for each part.
  3. Press Download my feedback.
  4. Email the file back to us.

Good to know: these are ideas for discussion. Products and prices are samples, pictures marked “temporary” are not the shop’s, and nothing here is live or final.

  • Concept 1

    The Deli Counter

    A New York deli window: red, green and cream, striped awning, price tags. Special idea: take a numbered ticket and be sent to the right counter.

    Try it: press the red Take a number button.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 2

    The Tap Room

    A craft taproom: dark slate, brass, a chalkboard tap list. Special idea: pull the tap handle and pour a pint.

    Try it: drag the handle down, or press Pour a pint.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 3

    The Shop Front

    Bright, friendly and drawn: the page is a little illustrated shop. Special idea: the Open/Closed sign is real, and the door opens into the shop.

    Try it: tap the shop door, or press Let me in.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 4

    The Hamper Box

    Calm and gift-led: linen, sage, blush and fine lines. Special idea: build a hamper and write the gift tag.

    Try it: scroll to Build a hamper and tap some items.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
  • Concept 5

    The Local

    A proud neighbourhood shop: forest green, ledger and noticeboard, tartan-style stripes. Special idea: a stamp card for regulars (an idea for the Members area).

    Try it: scroll to Members and tap the next stamp slot.
    Open conceptReview and give feedback
