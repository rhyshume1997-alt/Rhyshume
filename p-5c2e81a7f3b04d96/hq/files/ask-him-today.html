Ask him, full version

Date: 25 November 2026. Tick as you go. Never ask for passwords, card details or private keys. Access comes through invites (Shopify collaborator request, registrar invite). Tokens go straight into Vercel, not by message.

1. Ask him first (these block everything)

  • [ ] Grant: Has it been approved? When do you expect to hear? (Nothing paid starts before.)
  • [ ] Quote: Is he happy for the job to be the custom storefront (not just “connect the existing shop”)? If yes, the price changes and needs a new written quote. The £2,000 stays as is until he agrees.
  • [ ] Pay in full or monthly? Tick on the contract.
  • [ ] Customer accounts: Yes at launch? (He said yes today, confirm it’s in writing.)
  • [ ] Premises licence: Does it cover off-sales (selling to take away)? Does it cover selling online, click and collect and delivery? Can he send a copy of the licence and the operating plan?
  • [ ] Hours: Confirm the 10am to 10pm rule for payment. Is that the licensed off-sales hours? What happens to an order placed at 11pm (held until morning)?
  • [ ] Age checks: Who checks ID at collection? What does he want for delivery (courier checks, or no alcohol delivery at launch)?
  • [ ] Delivery: Where will he deliver (Milngavie only, Glasgow, all Scotland, UK)? Which courier? Does he want alcohol delivery at launch, or collection only? (Default: off until verified.)
  • [ ] Dubai font: Does he own a licence for web use? (Answer so far: no, use a similar one.)
  • [ ] Care plan: £60 a month on the old quote. Does it still apply?

2. Get from him (access and files)

  • [ ] Which apps are installed (POS, DNA till connector, shipping, age check)
  • [ ] Is click and collect (local pickup) switched on? Which location?

  • [ ] Do products already have descriptions, photos, ABV and sizes?
  • [ ] Later, when you are building: he creates a Storefront access token and puts it in Vercel with you, not in a message

  • [ ] Who looks after the DNA system? Contact for support?

4. Legal checks to raise (not legal advice)

Ask if he has (or can get from his solicitor or licensing board):

  • [ ] Premises licence and operating plan (above)
  • [ ] Rules for selling alcohol online and by delivery in his area
  • [ ] Minimum unit pricing on every alcohol product (it must meet the Scottish rate)
  • [ ] How he records deliveries (what, where, when, who received)
  • [ ] A courier who will check age at the door
  • [ ] Where he is allowed to send alcohol to
  • [ ] Privacy notice and cookie choice (you can draft, he approves)
  • [ ] Allergen information shown for food
