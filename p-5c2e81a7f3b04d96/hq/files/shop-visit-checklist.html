Jack O’Beers & Deli: shop visit checklist

13 Station Road, Milngavie, G62 8PG. Open Wednesday to Monday, 11am to 7pm (Tuesday not confirmed). Tick as you go. Everything marked SLOT replaces a placeholder on the site, so name files exactly as shown.

0. Before you go (10 minutes)

1. Film (shoot in this order, landscape, 4K if you can)

Keep every clip steady, 10 to 20 seconds, no talking, no music playing in the shop (licensing), no other brands’ logos in shot if you can help it.

# Shot Where it goes Notes
1 Hero loop: a pint being poured at the tap, side-on, glass fills with foam head Home page hero (SLOT replaces pour.mp4/webm) Dark background behind, light from the side, 10 s. Bottle entering from out of frame also works.
2 Shop walk-through: slow move from the door along the shelves and the deli counter Home / About Gimbal or very slow walk. Avoid customers’ faces.
3 Deli counter at work: cheese being cut, pie lifted, board assembled Deli page Hands only, close-up, 10 s each.
4 Keg or keezer pour: tap handle pulled, beer into glass Kegs / keezer rental Same angle as the keezer photos in section 2.
5 A hamper being packed: items placed in, lid on, ribbon Gift hampers Overhead is best.
6 Click & collect handover: an order bag passed over the counter (staff only) Click & collect page Hands and bag only.
7 Shop front: the sign, the door opening, the street (daylight) About / Contact 10 s locked off, then a slow push in.
8 Mascot / logo moments: the logo sign, any bull artwork, merch, signage Logo animation reference Photograph and film it so the animator can match the look.
9 Optional 30 s owner clip: “Who we are” in their own words (phone, quiet corner) About page Only if they want to. Ask what they would say, do not script claims.

2. Photograph

2a. The scroll backgrounds (most important, needs the tripod)

Beers page: “pint empties as you scroll”: six photos of the same pint glass in the same spot, nothing moved between frames. - [ ] Tripod, locked focus, locked exposure, locked white balance, 3:2 landscape, at least 1920 px wide (ideally 4000 px) - [ ] Same glass, same bar or table, same background (dim warm bar backdrop works well), light from the same side - [ ] Take 6 frames: beer-1 full with foam head, beer-2 about 80%, beer-3 about 60%, beer-4 about 40%, beer-5 about 20%, beer-6 empty with foam lacing on the glass. Sip or pour between frames, don’t touch the camera. - [ ] Take a few extras of each stage (they can pick the best) - [ ] Wipe fingerprints and condensation as needed, keep some condensation, it looks great

Keezer rental: “glass fills as you scroll”: same method, the keezer with taps and a glass underneath. - [ ] keezer-1 empty glass under the tap, then 5 stages to keezer-6 full pint with head. Taps, wood panel and drip tray identical in all six. - [ ] Also ask: what exactly is the keezer (chest freezer converted to taps?), how many taps, which kegs fit

3. Design decisions to settle with them (show the preview on your phone)

4. Information to collect (copy straight into a table, one row per product)

For each product: name, short description, style or type, ABV, size, price, stock, allergens (from the label), tasting notes, “goes well with”, producer/brewery, photo file names, which category (beer, cider, wine, keg, hamper, deli). Do not guess ABV or allergens; take them from the label or the supplier sheet.

5. Questions to ask (and write the answer next to each)

The business - [ ] Trading name: “Jack O’Beers” or “Jack O’ Beers & Deli”? (the email signature says “Jack O’ Beers & Deli”) - [ ] Opening hours, including Tuesday (we only know Wednesday to Monday, 11am to 7pm), bank holidays, Christmas - [ ] Phone and email to publish (we have 07405 480950 and sales@jackobeers.com) - [ ] Their story, how long open, who is behind it, what makes them different, what they do not do - [ ] Event enquiries: what events, capacity, prices, who handles them

Selling online (Shopify) - [ ] Is the Shopify store live? Which plan? Can they add a Storefront API token (steps are in the README), or set up with you present? Never share passwords by message. - [ ] Do the till and Shopify stock stay in sync? Which till? (the workbook says DNA) - [ ] Which payment methods are on? Are Apple Pay and Google Pay enabled? - [ ] Taxes, VAT number display, and which products are age-restricted

Click & collect - [ ] Days, time windows, lead time (we assume Wednesday to Monday, 11am to 7pm in three windows), how many orders per slot, who may collect, ID check at collection, what happens if an item is out of stock

Shipping and alcohol (licensing, handle carefully) - [ ] Their premises licence: does it cover off-sales (they said yes), delivery, and distance sales? Ask to see the operating plan and the licence conditions - [ ] Which courier, which countries (UK only, or international “where possible”), costs and times, packaging for glass, age check on delivery and who does it - [ ] Minimum order, free delivery threshold, returns and damaged goods policy, refunds on alcohol - [ ] Age verification at checkout: a self-declaration tick only, or something stronger?

Keezer / kegs - [ ] What it is exactly, how many kegs per unit, what they rent (keezer, kegs, gas, glasses?), price, deposit, dates, delivery and collection, who sets up, insurance, damage rules, minimum age of hirer

Marketing and legal - [ ] Do they want to show customers or staff? Who signs the photo permission? - [ ] No content aimed at children. Confirm the audience is adults. - [ ] Privacy notice, terms of sale and returns text: who will check them (a solicitor or adviser)? - [ ] Domain: is it jackobeers.com, who controls DNS, and who hosts email?
