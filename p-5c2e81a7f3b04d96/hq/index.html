My job list

Before the meeting

  • Open “Ask him and get: 12 things” on my phone
  • Print or open the contract (Word and fillable PDF)
  • Check the concepts board loads on my phone
  • Charge the phone and the Pocket 3

At the meeting

  • Grant: approved yet? When will he hear?
  • Does he want the custom storefront? (needs a new quote)
  • Pay in full or monthly? Tick it on the contract
  • Show the five concepts and note what he likes
  • Get the Shopify invite (never his password)
  • Ask about the DNA till sync
  • Premises licence, hours and age checks
  • Logo as a vector file
  • Agree a shoot day

After

  • Send the revised quote
  • Send the contract for signing
  • Write up his answers and what is still missing
  • Solicitor to check the contract
  • Do not start work until the grant is approved and it is signed
