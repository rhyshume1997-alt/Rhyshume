1. Ask him and get

Never ask for passwords. Access comes by invite.

Ask

  • Has he accepted the Council's Offer of Grant? (Approved in principle 24 Nov, DSG108, £2,000.)
  • Has the Council been told about the custom storefront and the price? (It has to be before work starts.)
  • Is he happy with the custom storefront and the new quote?
  • Pay in full or monthly? (The grant pays him back after, so paying in full means he can claim sooner.)
  • Does his licence cover selling to take away, online and delivery? What hours?
  • Who checks age at collection and delivery? Alcohol delivery at launch, or collection only?
  • Does the DNA till stock sync with Shopify?

Get

  • Shopify collaborator code (4 digits) and store address
  • Where is his domain (GoDaddy?) and does it show his Shopify shop today?
  • The logo as a vector file, the product list with allergens, and a shoot day
2. Hook it all up
Nothing live until the grant is approved, the contract is signed and he says yes in writing.

Before launch

  • Request Shopify store access with his store address and 4-digit code. He approves. (Apps and products only, never payments.)
  • In his Shopify add the Headless channel and copy the public Storefront token. (If there is no Headless channel, make a custom app instead.)
  • In Vercel make a new project from the site's GitHub repo.
  • Add SHOPIFY_STORE_DOMAIN and SHOPIFY_STOREFRONT_TOKEN under Environment Variables, then redeploy.
  • Test on the preview: real products, add to basket, press Checkout, land on Shopify. Stop. Do not pay.

Launch day

  • With his written yes, sit with him at GoDaddy. Screenshot the current DNS records first.
  • In Vercel add his domain and the www one. Copy the two records it shows.
  • In GoDaddy change the @ A record and the www CNAME. Remove parked or forwarding. Do not touch MX (email).
  • Wait for the green tick and padlock in Vercel. Open it on your phone.
  • Test email to and from his address, one basket to Shopify checkout, write down who holds which login.

3. Film and photograph today

Clips 10 to 20 seconds, no talking, no music. Keep the important part in the middle of wide shots. Full shoot list

  • Set both cameras. iPhone: 4K, 25 fps, HDR Video off. Pocket 3: 4K, 25 fps, Normal colour, shutter 1/50.
  • iPhone, WIDE, tripod: the pint in 6 stages, full to empty (beer-1 to beer-6).
  • iPhone, WIDE, tripod: the keezer in 6 stages, empty to full (keezer-1 to keezer-6).
  • iPhone, SQUARE: every product, front on.
  • iPhone, SQUARE: every gift hamper, from above.
  • iPhone, WIDE: shop front, inside, deli counter.
  • Pocket 3, WIDE, tripod: a pour into a glass, Slow Motion 4K 120 fps, 3 takes (the hero clip).
  • Pocket 3, WIDE, 4K 25 fps: walk through the shop (Follow mode).
  • Pocket 3, WIDE, 4K 25 fps: deli counter hands only, a hamper being packed from above, shop front in daylight.
