Temporary parts

  • Wordmark: plain text. The real logo (a smiling bull in a “Jack O’Beers & Deli” badge, sent separately as an image) is not in this build, because the file was not available to save. Save it as jack-logo.png in this folder and it appears in the header. Note the logo says “& Deli”; the workbook names the business “Jack O’Beers”. Confirm the trading name.
  • Colours: placeholders. They are not sampled from the logo. Replace with the real values.
  • Font: Dubai is a Microsoft font. Its web embedding licence has not been checked and nothing was downloaded, so temporary Archivo and Newsreader (open licence, already hosted in this repository) are used. Decision recorded: confirm the Dubai licence or pick an open alternative.
  • Imagery: the bottle, glass and scene are drawn illustrations, labelled as such. They are not photographs or footage.
  • Products, flavours, pairings, categories: all sample data, labelled Demo. Prices and allergens show as to be confirmed.

Functional requirements needing agreement

  • Online purchasing: which products are sold online, and whether the till and shop stock stay in step
  • Click and collect: slots, lead time, stock, who is allowed to collect
  • Shipping: areas, carriers, packaging, cost rules, alcohol delivery restrictions
  • Hampers: fixed or build-your-own, pricing, packaging
  • Equipment rental: what “keyser units” means, what is rented, the notes about two kegs, charging “for what they use” and customers choosing their dates. None of this is assumed. Needs a conversation before anything is built.
  • Membership: what members get, accounts, personal data, sign-up

Decisions before a quote and formal specification

  1. Shopify theme or separate front end (see below)
  2. Which of the requested services are in the first release
  3. Logo, colours and font confirmed; trading name confirmed
  4. Who supplies photography, video, product data and copy
  5. How age is checked and how alcohol sales and delivery are handled, once the client has confirmed their licensing position
  6. What the till integration needs to do, if anything
  7. Domain, hosting and ongoing care arrangements

Shopify implementation considerations

The client already lists Shopify. A separate custom storefront is not assumed to be the best answer.

Customised Shopify theme

  • Keeps checkout, accounts, orders, tax, shipping and stock in one place the client may already use
  • Most of this design (layout, type, colour, flavour pages, deli cards, the opening animation) can be built in a theme with product metafields for flavour, pairing and allergen data
  • Click and collect and shipping are standard Shopify features, subject to plan and apps
  • Limits: the lying-to-standing opening and the cursor are front-end work inside theme code, fine but needs care for speed; rental and membership logic usually need apps

Separate front end on Shopify’s APIs

  • More freedom for animation and bespoke flows
  • More to build, host and maintain; checkout and account behaviour need extra care; the client depends on a developer for changes
  • Worth it only if the theme cannot deliver something the client truly needs

Leaning (to confirm): start with a customised theme. Dependencies: Shopify plan and store status (the workbook says no website, yet lists Shopify, so what exists is unconfirmed), access arranged safely, DNA till integration options, and the client’s alcohol compliance answers. No Shopify account was connected, and no data, orders or credentials were touched.

Known technical and compliance risks

  • Alcohol: licensing conditions, age verification, delivery restrictions, permitted payment and sale processes, advertising standards, privacy and accounts all need checking with the client and their advisers. Nothing here claims any set-up satisfies Scottish licensing.
  • Marketing: the audience includes young families, but alcohol promotion must not target or appeal to children. The visuals avoid child-focused imagery.
  • Product information: allergens and ABV must come from verified sources
  • Illustrations stand in for photography; real imagery changes the feel and the page weight
  • Heavy animation and video must stay fast on phones; reduced motion is respected
  • Till and Shopify stock sync is an unknown
  • Dubai font licence unchecked
  • The preview lives in the Rhys Hume repository under an unguessable, noindex address. It is private by obscurity only, not password protected.
