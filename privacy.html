What happens to what you send me

Who I am

I'm Rhys Hume, based in Glasgow, and you can reach me at rhys@rhyshume.co.uk or on 07908 041480

What the forms collect

  • The contact form collects your name, company, email, phone number if you give one, the service you're asking about and your message
  • The council digital grants form collects your name, company, email, phone number if you give one, your council area and what you want built

How form messages reach me

The forms are handled by Formspree, a form service, which receives what you type and passes it to me by email, so Formspree processes your details on my behalf, see Formspree's privacy policy

Booking a chat

The book a chat button opens a Google Calendar appointment page, so anything you enter there is handled by Google and shared with me so we can have the chat

Fonts

The site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads

What I use it for

I use what you send to reply to your enquiry and to set up the chat or the work you ask about

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it, by emailing rhys@rhyshume.co.uk
