Privacy

What happens to what you send me.

1.0Who I am

I’m Rhys Hume, based in Glasgow, and you can reach me at rhyshume1997@gmail.com or on 07908 041480.

2.0What the forms collect

  • The contact form collects your name, company, email, phone number if you give one, the service you’re asking about and your message.
  • The council digital grants form collects your name, company, email, phone number if you give one, your council area and what you want built.

3.0How form messages reach me

When you send a form, this website passes what you typed to me by email through Resend, an email delivery service. Resend processes your details on my behalf. See Resend’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

4.0Booking a chat

The “book a chat” button opens a Google Calendar appointment page. Anything you enter there is handled by Google and shared with me so we can have the chat.

5.0Fonts

The fonts are served from this website, so loading a page doesn’t send anything to Google Fonts.

6.0What I use it for

I use what you send to reply to your enquiry and to set up the chat or the work you ask about.

7.0How long I keep it

I keep enquiries for 12 months, then delete them.

8.0Why I’m allowed to use it

My lawful basis is legitimate interests: replying to your enquiry.

9.0ICO registration

I’m not currently registered with the ICO.

10.0Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it, by emailing rhyshume1997@gmail.com.
