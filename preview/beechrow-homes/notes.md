## What changed and why

1. The phone number (01236 725 077) and a Get a quote button sit at the top of the page, and on a phone a call bar stays at the bottom of the screen. Why: someone on a phone can ring or ask for a quote without scrolling.
2. The main text goes from about 252 words on their home page to about 139 words, with 5 services taken from their own pages. Why: shorter text reads better on a phone, and nothing is added that they have not said.
3. A demo form called "Get a quote" takes drawings and photos and posts nowhere. Why: it shows how an enquiry could look without collecting anything from anyone.
