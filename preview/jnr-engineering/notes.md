## What changed and why

1. The phone number (0141 647 5913) and a Get a quote button sit at the top of the page. Why: someone on a phone can ring or ask for a quote without scrolling.
2. The main text goes from about 556 words on their home page to about 171 words, with 5 services taken from their own pages. Why: shorter text reads better on a phone, and nothing is added that they have not said.
