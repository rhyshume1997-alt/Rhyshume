What we do

  • Metal Fabrication

    Frames, guards and one off assemblies in mild steel, stainless or aluminium, welded to your drawing.

  • CNC Milling

    Down to ±0.01mm on a 5 axis machine with a 1200 × 750 bed, from one prototype to a full batch.

  • CNC Turning

    Up to a metre across and 3 metres long, or up to 5 metres for slimmer work under Ø160.

  • Water Jet Profiling

    Profiles cut from sheet and plate. It is a cold cut, so there is no heat distortion at the edge.

  • Inspection

    First article reports, calibrated gauging and full dimensional checks, with paperwork supplied if your job needs it.
