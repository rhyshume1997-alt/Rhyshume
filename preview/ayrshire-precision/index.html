What we do

  • Machining

    We machine non-ferrous alloys, steels, stainless steel, duplex and heat-resistant alloys.

    Round parts up to 1200mm dia., 2 metres between centres

  • Heavy duty machining

    We are used to heavy duty machining and metal removal, and to machining smaller parts.

  • Quality, inspection and testing

    Parts are checked in-process and at final inspection, using handheld tools and a CNC CMM.

  • Programming

    Offline and on-machine programming by trained, skilled machinists.

About

Ayrshire Precision was first established in 1969 and is based in Coylton, South Ayrshire. Its skilled team includes many who came through the four-year Apprenticeship Programme.
