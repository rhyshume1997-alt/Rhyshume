# The six looks

Pick the look from the person's answers, in this order: **audience → personality → how the app looks**. Tell them the choice and the reason in one line.

| Look | `style` | Feels | Choose it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soft clay | `clay` | warm, premium, Pixar-short polish | the default for consumer apps: money, health, habits, kids, family, anything that should feel safe and kind |
| Plush | `plush` | cosy, tactile, gently funny | wellness, sleep, journaling, social, cafes, gifting: things you'd want to hug |
| Fuzzy | `fuzzy` | lovable, cheeky, a bit chaotic | music, games, community, creator tools, brands that want to be loud and huggable |
| Designer toy | `vinyl` | cool, collectible, made with attitude | dev tools, AI assistants, fintech for young people, streetwear-ish brands, anything that wants to be cool more than cute |
| Flat line | `line` | graphic, confident, editorial | brands with a strong flat identity or big type, packaging, web-first products, when the UI itself is flat |
| Retro sticker | `sticker` | playful, nostalgic, rebellious | social, games, creator tools, brands that want personality over polish |

## Quick rules

- **Kids, families, health, money** → clay or plush. Trust beats edge.
- **Professionals and developers** → vinyl or line. Cute reads as unserious; crafted reads as competent.
- **A dark UI** → clay, vinyl or fuzzy (they glow on dark); line and sticker want light backgrounds.
- **Tiny sizes** (menu bar, widgets, notifications) → clay or line; fur and fabric lose detail under 48 px.
- **The app already has an illustration style** → match its flatness: flat UI art → line or sticker; 3D icons → clay or vinyl.
- When two fit, choose the one that **differs most from their competitors' mascots**.

## What each look needs in the brief

- **clay**: smooth, simple forms. Name the material colour ("warm coral"). Glossy bean eyes come with the look.
- **plush**: describe it as a toy that could be sewn (no thin parts, no sharp points). Felt details (beak, ears, patches) read beautifully.
- **fuzzy**: one fur colour plus one accessory (headphones, a horn colour). Faces stay simple: small eyes, a mouth.
- **vinyl**: oversized head, compact body, one accessory with crisp edges (backpack, antenna, glasses). Two to three paint colours.
- **line**: one bold flat body shape in the brand colour; everything else is black line (arms, legs, face). Shapes that work as a logo.
- **sticker**: off-white plus one spot colour, rubber-hose limbs, white gloves and sneakers suit it.
